## 图片和贴纸动画

- 在时间线上给图片和贴纸添加关键帧，分别设置位置、大小、旋转和透明度。灵录会在关键帧之间自动补间，预览和导出都应用动画。
- 播放控制栏可以添加关键帧。选中图片或贴纸后，也可以用快捷键在关键帧之间跳转。

## 提词器

- 用一个按钮开始、暂停和继续滚动。跟读暂时不可用时，提词器可以按设定速度滚动。

## Picture and sticker animation

- Add timeline keyframes for position, size, rotation, and opacity. Capwise animates between keyframes in both the preview and the exported video.
- Add keyframes from the playback controls and use shortcuts to jump between keyframes on the selected picture or sticker.

## Teleprompter

- Start, pause, and resume scrolling with one button. The teleprompter can scroll at a steady pace when voice following is temporarily unavailable.
