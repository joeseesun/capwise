## 中文

- 字幕面板新增「重新生成字幕」。可以用上次模型或换模型，只重做检测到的异常段或重做整段视频。
- 新字幕会先与原音核对，可逐条试听，再确认替换。替换前会备份原字幕，也可以一步撤销。使用 Qwen 等模型时无需额外安装 Whisper。
- 修复本地 Qwen 偶发复读、把大量短字幕挤在一起的问题。遇到异常输出时，灵录会尝试分段重转写。
- 改善单行字幕输入的响应。文字在确认或失焦时一次提交，一步撤销可恢复整行。

## English

- Regenerate captions directly from the captions panel. Reuse the previous model or choose another, and regenerate detected problem areas or the entire video.
- New captions are checked against the recording. Listen to each audio cue before applying the preview. Capwise backs up the previous captions, and one undo restores them. Qwen and other models require no separate Whisper download.
- Fixed an issue where local Qwen could repeat passages and create a dense stretch of very short captions. Capwise retries suspicious output in smaller audio chunks.
- Editing a single caption is more responsive. Changes are applied together when you confirm the edit or leave the field, and one undo restores the entire line.
