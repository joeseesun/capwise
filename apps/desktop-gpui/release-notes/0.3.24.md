### 简体中文

- 在编辑器里边看画面边录配音。录制时音轨实时显示波形，停止后自动落轨，可撤销或重录。
- 根据录音电平自动补偿偏轻的配音，为峰值留出余量。音量可以继续在片段属性里调整。

### English

- Record a voiceover while watching the video. The timeline shows the live waveform, and stopping places the take on the audio track automatically. You can undo or record a new take.
- Quiet voiceovers get automatic gain with room for audio peaks. You can adjust the gain in the clip settings.
