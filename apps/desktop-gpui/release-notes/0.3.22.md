- 在时间线点「添加音乐」，可搜索并试听在线配乐。点「＋」将完整音轨存入工程。
- 配乐按歌名、作者、风格和用途搜索。内置配乐继续支持离线使用。
- 灵录与官网共用配乐库，新曲目上架后无需更新应用。

### English

- Open Add Music in the timeline to search and preview online tracks. Select + to save the full track to the project.
- Search by title, artist, style, or use. Built-in tracks remain available offline.
- Capwise and its website share a music library. New tracks appear without an app update.
