### English

- Open Add Music in the timeline to search and preview online tracks. Select + to save the full track to the project and fit the clip to the video.
- Search by title, artist, style, or use. The music picker opens to built-in tracks for offline use.
- Capwise and its website share a music library. New tracks appear without an app update.
