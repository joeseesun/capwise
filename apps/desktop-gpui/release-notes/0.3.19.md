## 中文

- 优化字幕文字编辑。输入时保留本地草稿，失焦或确认后再更新预览和保存项目。
- 修复拖拽字幕条边缘或在右侧输入起止时间后，修改有时无法保存的问题。重新打开项目和导出视频时会使用修改后的字幕时间。
- 优化长字幕轨道和拖动关键帧时的预览响应，减少复杂时间线在预览和导出时的重复计算。
- macOS 并行查询录制设备，缩短主窗口打开后等待设备就绪的时间。

## English

- Caption text stays in the input while you type. The preview and project update when you leave the field or confirm the edit.
- Fixed a bug that could discard changes made by dragging a caption edge or entering start and end times in the sidebar. Reopened projects and exports use the edited caption timing.
- Improved preview responsiveness on long caption tracks and during keyframe drags, and reduced repeated timeline calculations during preview and export.
- macOS now checks recording devices in parallel so they're ready sooner after the main window opens.
